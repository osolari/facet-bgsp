\subsection{Claim-strength policy}
Algebraic identities are presented with complete proofs. Deterministic, statistical, systems, and application claims are labeled separately. A target theorem remains a conjecture or proof program until every dependency in the registry is resolved. Negative results and failed assumptions remain in the final evidence record.
